Reuse matrix

What v1 takes from Dom's repos, from where, and how. Copy means lift the files and edit. Borrow means rebuild from the idea. New means no source exists. All paths are under /home/roboto/devel/ and were checked to exist on 16 October 2026. Line counts are from wc -l. Everything except OpenClaw is Dom's own code; OpenClaw is MIT and only borrowed from.

Two rules for anyone copying. First, a copied file comes in with its tests, or it does not come in. Second, every file loses its source repo's config, logger and db imports at the door and takes them as parameters, which is the repo's service rule anyway.
