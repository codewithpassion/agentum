The builder reserves an address before the agent exists, and works on a `draft` agent row that becomes the real agent on "Create agent". The mailbox itself is V5's.

## Scope
- `packages/services/src/agents.ts`: `suggestAddress(slug)` (the slug, or slug-2 and so on when taken), `checkAddress(principal, local)` (syntax: lowercase letters, digits, dots and dashes, 2 to 40 characters; a reserved-word list such as postmaster, abuse, admin, noreply; and availability), and `reserveAddress(principal, agentId, local)`, which writes `email_local`, maps the unique violation to `ServiceError("conflict", "That address is taken")`, and logs `agent.address_reserved`.
- `createDraft(principal, values)` inserts a `draft` agent. `activate(principal, agentId)` turns a draft into `active` and logs `agent.created`. `discardDraft` archives it, because its dry-run events keep it referenced.
- `list`, `findAgent` for runs and triggers, Slack routing and the desk skip drafts. Dry runs may still start on a draft (V3.7).
- TRPC: `agents.checkAddress` and `agents.reserveAddress` in `agents-router.ts`.
