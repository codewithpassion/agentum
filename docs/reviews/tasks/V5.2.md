## What was decided

Option A. Port the mailbox logic into `packages/mailbox/` inside Harbourmaster's Worker, with one per-mailbox Durable Object (`MailboxDO`, migration tag `v6`) holding the parsed body, and the raw MIME and attachments in `FILES`. No change in agentic-inbox-hei. The doc gives the nine `MailboxPort` methods and the type shapes for V5.1, the inbound path (`email()` export, D1 lookup, `setReject` for unknown or oversize mail), the provisioning path (literal per-address rule, action `worker`, `findRule` first, `CF_EMAIL_ROUTING_TOKEN`, `MAIL_ZONE_ID`, `MAIL_WORKER_NAME`, 200 rules per domain), sending, wrangler placement, and the licence handling.

## Ideas beyond the scope

- A Durable-Object-free variant (D1 index, bodies in `FILES`) would remove the DO class, tag `v6` and the bun:sqlite adapter. The doc lists it under "For Dom to confirm" because the `MailboxPort` hides the choice.
- Email Sending publishes `message.bounced` and related events through Queues event subscriptions. If bounces do not arrive as mail (the `cf-bounce` MX suggests they do not), V5.7 may need a Queue binding and a `queue()` handler. V5.12 should look at a real bounce first.
- Cloudflare's Wrangler `addresses` field manages Email Routing rules for a Worker from config. It does not fit per-agent runtime provisioning, but it is a neat way to manage the fixed addresses of a deployment.
- Past about 150 agents on one domain, a catch-all on the apex with a D1 registry and `setReject` is the way out of the 200-rule limit. That changes how the domain treats unknown addresses, so it needs Dom.
