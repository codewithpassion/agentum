## Nits left

- The loader fetches only the agents. The desk itself loads in the browser, because "today" starts at the viewer's local midnight, so the server render shows "Loading the desk…". This differs from the scope line "loader calls `desk.get`" on purpose.
- `usePolledQuery` has no guard against responses that arrive out of order. A tick that started before a filter change or an answer can land after it and show stale cards for up to 10 s.
- Coming back to a tab fires both `focus` and `visibilitychange`, so the desk loads twice.
- Every 10 s the desk reads `desk.get`, `requests.pending` and `skillStore.proposals`, and the sidebar reads `desk.needsYouCount`. Needs you data is read several times per cycle.
- The desk loader fetches `agents.list` again although the `/_app` layout loader has it. `agents.list({})` skips archived agents, so their cards read "An agent".
- The sidebar badge waits for its own poll after an answer on the desk, up to 10 s.
- "+ New task" ignores the agent filter and picks the first agent.
- After a filter change the old cards stay on screen until the new data arrives, with no loading state.
- "See changes" for a trigger or routine goes to the list page, and for a skill to the skill page, not to the proposed item or version.
- A conflict on answering (someone answered first) is swallowed without a message, and the board refreshes.
