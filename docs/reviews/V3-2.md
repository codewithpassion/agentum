The test checks two grants, no `runtimeConfig`, both folders on `agentProfile.profile`, and a failing grant named in the error.

Nothing else in V3 broke. Routers stay thin, services take their dependencies in `composeWith`, there are no schema or migration changes, and the new file came with its test.
